THE IDEA

Last in. First out.

A stack follows one simple rule: the last item you add is the first item you remove.

01
↑

Push

Add each word to the top of the stack. Every new word sits above the previous one.

stack.push(word)
02
↓

Pop

Remove words one by one from the top. The most recently added word comes out first.

word = stack.pop()
03
✓

Reversed

Collect the popped words in order. The output is now the exact reverse of the input.

output.push(word)
